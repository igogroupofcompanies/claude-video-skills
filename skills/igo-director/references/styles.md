# Style guide

| Style | Pacing | Grade | Type / motion | Audio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cinematic | slow, long takes 4–8 s, slow push-ins | teal/orange or warm filmic, lifted blacks, grain, vignette, 2.39:1 optional | minimal serif titles, long fades | score-led, wide dynamics |
| Documentary | observational, 3–6 s, interview + B-roll | natural, slightly desaturated | lower thirds, quiet captions | VO + ambience, ducked music |
| Corporate | steady, 3–4 s scenes | brand palette, clean, high key | sans-serif, accent rule, count-up stats | light bed, VO -16 LUFS |
| Editorial | rhythmic, mixed shot sizes | contrasty, designed palette | big type, grid layouts | music-driven |
| Commercial | fast 1–3 s, hero product close-ups | punchy, saturated, polished | bold kinetic type, CTA end card | beat-synced, -14 LUFS |
| Social | hook in first 3 s, cut every 1–2 s, vertical | bright, high contrast | large type (≥ 6% of width), captions always | sound-off safe, -14 LUFS |

Defaults: 30 fps (24 for cinematic), yuv420p, AAC 48 kHz, safe margins 5% (10% on vertical platforms' UI zones).
